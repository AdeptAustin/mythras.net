# Third Party Publishers

---
## What's Third Party?

Any good system has a community creating even more for it. _Mythras_  is no different. This page is dedicated to publishers aside from _The Design Mechanism_ that sell even more _Mythras_ content whether that be rules supplements, adventures, or even entire new games based on the _Mythras_ ruleset.

---
## Aeon Games

_Aeon Games_ is dedicated to publishing affordable and innovative roleplaying games. They've _Perceforest_, a setting for high chivalry, as well as _Weird of Hali_, a game for playing in the world of H.P. Lovecraft with a twist.

## FrostByte Books

Run by Clarence Redd, _FrostByte Books_ is a small publishing company based in the south of Sweden. _FrostByte Books_ has published games based on _Mythras_ taking it into the future as well as the 1920's. Also check out _TREY_ for solo roleplay and _The Comae Engine_ for a simplified, freeform roleplaying experience.

| Links | [Web](https://www.frostbytebooks.com/) | [DTRPG](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/publisher/10544/frostbyte-books) |

## Rudus Publishing

Rudus, meaning rubble in Latin, is the name under which Rich LeDuc publishes _Rubble and Ruin_. Based on _Mythras Imperative_, R&R tries to capture the feel of old-school fantasy gaming in a science fiction, post-apocalyptic world where science and technology replace magic.

| Links | [DTRPG](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/publisher/18532/rudus-publishing) | [R&R](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/354244/rubble-and-ruin) |

## SpaceRoadie

_SpaceRoadie_ has put out many Pay What You Want (PWYW) products, the most intersting to _Mythras_ players probably being _Blood Junkies_. It started out as a _Mythras Imperative_ hack of _VTM_ and grew from there.

| Links | [DTRPG](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/publisher/16792/spaceroadie) | [Blood Junkies](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/519933/blood-junkies) |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |

## Black Lodge Games

_Black Lodge Games_ is on a mission from God to bring high level immersion and atmosphere to tabletop role playing games. No apologies, no compromises. _Cults of Zahak_ is their offering designed for _Mythras Imperative_.
