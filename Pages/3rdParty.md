# Third Party Publishers

---
## What's Third Party?

Any good system has a community creating even more for it. _Mythras_  is no different. This page is dedicated to publishers aside from _The Design Mechanism_ that sell even more _Mythras_ content whether that be rules supplements, adventures, or even entire new games based on the _Mythras_ ruleset.

---
## Aeon Games

Aeon Games is dedicated to publishing affordable and innovative roleplaying games. They've _Perceforest_, a setting for high chivalry, as well as _Weird of Hali_, a game for playing in the world of H.P. Lovecraft with a twist.

## FrostByte Books

Run by Clarence Redd, _FrostByte Books_ is a small publishing company based in the south of Sweden. _FrostByte Books_ has published games based on _Mythras_ taking it into the future as well as the 1920's. Also check out _TREY_ for solo roleplay and _The Comae Engine_ for a simplified, freeform roleplaying experience.

| Links | [Web](https://www.frostbytebooks.com/) | [DTRPG](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/publisher/10544/frostbyte-books) |
